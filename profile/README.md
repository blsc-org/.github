# Bulgarian Language & Script Corpus (BLSC)

**Preserving Bulgarian linguistic heritage through open data**

**BLSC** is an independent, open initiative dedicated to collecting, preserving, structuring, and studying Bulgarian written language across historical periods (18th–20th centuries). The project focuses on historical orthography, script evolution, linguistic variation, and machine-readable textual resources.

---

### Research Areas
* **Historical Texts & Orthographic Traditions** — Digitization and preservation of original Bulgarian literary, administrative, and archival sources.
* **Pre- and Post-1945 Spelling Conventions** — Comparative representation of Ivanchevski orthography, BDS standards, and modern Bulgarian.
* **Linguistic Metadata & Corpus Annotation** — Structured JSON schemas with chronological, regional, and bibliographic descriptors.
* **Text Normalization & Original-Script Preservation** — Multi-layered architecture keeping raw diplomatic transcriptions alongside normalized texts and PDF facsimiles.
* **Open Datasets** — Machine-readable resources designed for computational linguistics, NLP, and digital humanities.
